## Findings

### LOW (copy consistency)

1. **The `/id/` footer still says "Ask" and "Arcade" in English.** This is the
   PRODUCT.md "two languages, one standard" principle.
   - The site already names both pages in Indonesian: `ask.title` is "Tanya
     situs ini" and `arcade.title` is "Gudang Mainan". Next to "Kontak" and
     "Dibuat dengan…" in the same footer, the English labels stand out.
   - "Now" and "Changelog" stay in English by the existing `nav.now` and
     `nav.changelog` keys, so they are consistent and not part of this finding.
   - Fix: add `nav.ask` ("Ask" / "Tanya") and `nav.arcade` ("Arcade" / "Gudang
     Mainan") and use them in the footer.

2. **"penjaringan calon peminjam" is a formal rendering of "loan lead
   generation".** Copy rhythm.
   - It is correct, but it reads like a report. In Indonesian product writing
     this is usually "akuisisi calon peminjam" or simply "lead pinjaman".
   - Fix: "…melayani akuisisi calon peminjam dalam skala besar." The numbers do
     not change.
