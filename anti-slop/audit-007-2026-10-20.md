## Findings

### LOW (copy rhythm)

1. **The phone line says "walk" twice in a row.** R-02: text must read
   naturally.
   - It reads "…a small valley you can walk. Walk the valley".
   - The link already says what you can do, so the sentence doesn't need to.
   - Fix:
     - EN: "Every page here is also a building in a small valley." + "Walk the
       valley".
     - ID: "Setiap halaman di sini juga sebuah bangunan di lembah kecil." +
       "Jelajahi lembah".

### LOW (layout rhythm)

2. **The phone link stretches its line.** Whitespace should be structural, not
   leftover.
   - The link is `inline-flex min-h-11` inside the sentence, so its 44px hit
     area makes the second line about 33px tall against the paragraph's 23px
     leading. At 390 the two lines look unevenly spaced.
   - Fix: give the link its own line under the sentence
     (`flex w-fit min-h-11 items-center`). It stays 44px, and the paragraph
     keeps even leading.
