## Findings

### MEDIUM (resilience)

1. **Without scripts, the valley asks the visitor to walk in and no longer says
   why they can't.** C-4: the UI holds up in every state.
   - The intro still reads "Every building here is a page on this site. Walk
     in, or pick from the list."
   - The line that explained the missing world ("The walkable valley appears
     here when scripts run. Every place in it is already in the list.") lived
     in the world's column, which the new `noscript` style hides.
   - Fix: render that same `game.loading` line under the intro, inside the list
     column, in a `<noscript><p>`. It adds about one line (24px) to the no-JS
     band, which stays inside the spec's "list plus padding". There is no new
     string.

### LOW (layout rhythm)

2. **Each card's top row leaves about 50px blank above the title.** Whitespace
   should be structural, not leftover.
   - The company logo is stacked under the date, so the right side of the row
     is about 100px tall while the badges on the left are about 30px.
   - The title starts below the logo, so the left half of that height is empty.
   - Fix: set the date and the logo side by side (`flex-row items-center`) in
     `FeaturedProjectCard.astro`. Each card gets about 50px shorter.

### LOW (copy, secondary audience)

3. **"front-end to services" is engineer shorthand in the sentence meant for
   freelance clients.**
   - R-02: text must feel natural to its reader. PRODUCT.md's freelance
     reader is judging "whether one person can deliver a whole system", and
     may not read "services" as the back end.
   - The ID "dari front-end sampai services" mixes in the same English term.
   - Fix:
     - EN: "Also open to freelance projects: a whole system, front end to back
       end, from one person."
     - ID: "Juga terbuka untuk proyek freelance: satu sistem utuh, dari
       front-end sampai back-end, dikerjakan satu orang."
     - No claim changes. The hero already names the microservices.
