## Findings

### LOW (layout rhythm)

1. **On a phone the case study's third number sits alone on its row.**
   Whitespace should be structural, not leftover.
   - The metric grid is two columns below `sm` and every case study has three
     numbers. At 390 the third tile takes half a row and leaves the other half
     empty, right where the reader is checking the proof.
   - Fix: three columns at every width (`grid-cols-3`, `MetricCounters.astro`),
     with tighter phone padding (`p-3 sm:p-5`). Measure that the labels wrap to
     no more than three lines at 360.

### LOW (consistency)

2. **The same evidence reads in two registers.** R-31 consistency.
   - The landing card sets "teams deploy independently" as 14px body text, by
     this change's own decision ("the metrics are the evidence").
   - The case study sets the same label as 12px mono uppercase tracked
     (`MetricCounters.astro`, the label span), the label register.
   - Fix: the case-study metric labels take the card treatment, `text-sm`
     muted body text in sentence case. The values stay mono.

### LOW (copy, ID)

3. **"Ganti mode gelap" reads as "replace dark mode".** R-02: text must read
   naturally.
   - The button switches dark mode on and off, and its sibling already reads
     "Nyalakan atau matikan suara".
   - Fix: "Nyalakan atau matikan mode gelap" (`ui.ts`, `nav.theme`). EN stays
     "Toggle dark mode".
