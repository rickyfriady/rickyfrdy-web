## Findings

### LOW (mobile layout)

1. **Metric values with a space can split across two lines.** R-03: text must
   not break apart on mobile.
   - "Rp 3 M+", "1,5 jt" and "5 min" hold a space. Nothing keeps them on one
     line in the card's stats row or in the case-study tile.
   - They fit at 360 today: measured in EN, ID and on both case studies, every
     value is one line. A longer value, or a narrower phone, would leave "Rp 3"
     on one line and "M+" on the next.
   - Fix: `whitespace-nowrap` on the value span in `FeaturedProjectCard.astro`
     and `MetricCounters.astro`.

### LOW (keyboard)

2. **Below `lg` the skip link skips one link.** C-1: every element should earn
   its place.
   - Below `lg` the valley band holds a single link ("Walk the valley"). There
     the skip link adds a Tab stop to save one.
   - At 1440 it saves 16 stops (measured: skip link at stop 18, first card next
     after Enter).
   - Fix: render the skip link from `lg` only (`hidden lg:block` on a wrapper,
     keeping `sr-only` until focus).
