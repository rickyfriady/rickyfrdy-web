## Findings

### HIGH (Hard Gate)

1. **The companion's dismiss fails contrast over the night valley.** R-25 and
   R-32 (non-text contrast of the focus indicator).
   - The button has no ground of its own, so its text and focus ring sit on
     whatever the page shows behind them.
   - In the light theme after 18:00 the valley band is night-dark (`#08120a`).
     There, the `[×]` (`--color-muted`, `#645142`) is 2.54:1 and the focus ring
     (`--color-accent`, `#05441d`) is 1.68:1. Both were measured with
     `contrast-check.py`.
   - Fix: give the button `bg-background` and a 2px `border-border` frame, so
     its text and ring are checked against its own ground, which already passes.

### MEDIUM (Purpose-Gate / honesty)

2. **The stage fallback promises something that won't happen.** R-38 / C-5.
   - "The valley opens here." is read mostly by visitors whose scripts are
     blocked, and for them it never opens.
   - Fix: "The walkable valley appears here when scripts run. Every place in it
     is already in the list." ID: "Lembah yang bisa dijelajahi muncul di sini
     saat skrip berjalan. Semua tempat di dalamnya sudah ada di daftar."

3. **The featured card title still glows on hover.** R-13.
   - `FeaturedProjectCard.astro:93` keeps
     `group-hover:[text-shadow:0_0_20px …accent 40%]`. That is the same soft
     glow this change removed from the card itself.
   - No purpose is written for it, and DESIGN.md's language is hard frames.
   - Fix: remove it. The hover already changes the title colour to `accent`.

4. **The proof is styled as secondary text.** C-1 / Part 3 (one focal point,
   hierarchical contrast).
   - The sentence the change exists for (employer, both tiers, numbers) is set
     in `text-muted text-sm`, the treatment for supporting copy.
   - On the first screen it reads as a caption to the name, not as the proof.
   - Fix: set it in `text-foreground` and keep the size. The name stays the
     focal point; the proof becomes the second read instead of the fourth.

### LOW (Quality Locks)

5. **The stage fallback uses a dashed border.** R-20 / R-31.
   - `border-dashed` is not in DESIGN.md's component table, and no reason is
     written for it.
   - Fix: use `soft-panel` (2px solid border, `surface` ground), the documented
     cheap frame.

6. **The EN proof line is one 36-word sentence.** Copy rhythm.
   - It holds two claims (front-end, services) for a reader who scans.
   - Fix: split it after the first claim. "At PT. Pegadaian, a state-owned
     lender, I led the Module Federation front-end that 5+ teams deploy on
     their own. I also designed the 9 NestJS microservices that replaced a
     legacy monolith with zero downtime."
   - ID likewise: "Di PT. Pegadaian, lembaga keuangan milik negara, saya
     memimpin front-end Module Federation yang di-deploy sendiri oleh 5+ tim.
     Saya juga merancang 9 microservice NestJS pengganti monolit lama tanpa
     downtime."
   - Every number is unchanged and still traced by `tests/pages/heroProof.test.ts`.
