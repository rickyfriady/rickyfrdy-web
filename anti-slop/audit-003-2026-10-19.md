## Findings

### HIGH (Hard Gate)

1. **The bottom nav covers the footer's links at the end of the page.** R-03,
   R-26.
   - The bar is fixed: 81px tall, 14px from the bottom. Nothing on the page
     leaves room for it.
   - Scrolled to the bottom, it sits on the footer's links and they cannot be
     tapped. At 390 it covers GitHub, LinkedIn, Ask, Now, Changelog and Arcade.
     At 1024 it covers LinkedIn, Ask, Now, Changelog and Arcade.
   - At 390 this already happened before this change. From 768 to 1279 it is
     new, because the bar now shows up to `xl`.
   - Fix: reserve the bar's height below `xl`. The footer gets `pb-28` (112px,
     on the 4px grid, which clears 81 + 14) and `xl:pb-8` restores today's
     padding where the bar is hidden.

### LOW (copy)

2. **"Tersedia untuk bekerja" reads like a translation.** PRODUCT.md, "two
   languages, one standard".
   - It is grammatical, but it mirrors "available for work" word for word.
   - Fix: "Terbuka untuk tawaran kerja", the way an Indonesian candidate would
     say it. The English stays as it is.
